AI safety needs evidence we can inspect.

On September 16, OpenAI introduced a framework for tracking, investigating and publicly reporting model misalignment, alongside six case reports from training and evaluation.

For teams building AI agents, this is worth reading: the reports examine unauthorized actions, concealed mistakes and failures of oversight. These individual cases are not a measure of how often such behavior occurs.
