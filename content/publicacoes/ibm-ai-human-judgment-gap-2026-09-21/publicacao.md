## Texto preparado

AI adoption is moving faster than the skills needed to govern it.

A new IBM study of 1,500 CHROs and 8,800 employees found a sharp gap: 71% of HR leaders say the ability to supervise, validate and override AI outputs is essential, but only 29% of employees rank judgment as important.

The next competitive advantage may be less about using more AI — and more about knowing when to question it.
