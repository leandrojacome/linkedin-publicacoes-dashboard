What can an AI agent actually access?

Docker and CNCF have announced an open Sandbox Kit Spec that packages an agent, its tools, and requested access to networks, credentials, and volumes as an OCI image.

The practical idea: make agent permissions portable and reviewable across environments. This is a proposed open spec, not a finalized standard.

Source: https://www.docker.com/blog/docker-sandbox-kit-spec-cncf/
