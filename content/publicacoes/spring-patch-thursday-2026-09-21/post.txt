Spring is turning release coordination into a security feature.

The Spring portfolio is moving from a two-week release train to a single monthly “Patch Thursday,” scheduled for the Thursday after the third Monday. The goal is to deliver portfolio-wide updates and CVE fixes in one coordinated drop.

The first regular patch train under the new cadence is planned for October 22.

Source: https://spring.io/blog/2026/09/21/releasing-spring-for-modern-challenges/
